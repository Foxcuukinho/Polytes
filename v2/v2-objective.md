When we say "v2", we are referring to all upcoming v1.x.0 releases.

# v2 Objective

The first version (v1) was like a fun MVP. Now, the goal of v2 is to get closer to creating a stickman that truly lives on your computer, like in Alan Becker's animations.

We hope to achieve this by evolving Polytes' three pillars:

- Animation and Physics
- Intelligence
- OS Integration

All three are equally necessary to achieve the v2 objective.

# Scope

The v2 scope consists of **20 subpillars and 36 sub-subpillars**, distributed across the three main pillars. Each subpillar and sub-subpillar will contain multiple features to be developed throughout the v2 releases.

Together, these subpillars, sub-subpillars, and their respective features define the full scope of v2.
