# **0:28–1:30 — Description**

"Introducing... Polytes!"

"As you just saw, Polytes is a desktop stickman pet heavily inspired by the animations of Alan Becker and his team."

"Our goal is to create a stickman that genuinely feels alive, living on your computer and making decisions like a living being would."

"We hope to achieve this without relying on generative AI for decision-making, but rather through an original system designed exclusively for Polytes' context."

"Polytes is still in v1.0.0-alpha, which means it's still **very** basic and may exhibit unexpected behavior on certain systems."

"And that's pretty much everything you can do in V1."

"Customize your stickman's color, name, and head type. These settings slightly influence their behavior."

"Grab any part of their body with your mouse, drag them around using real ragdoll physics, and throw them against windows, the edges of your screen, and other surfaces."

"You can even find and terminate your stickman as a process in Task Manager!"

"As for autonomous behavior, your stickman can:"
