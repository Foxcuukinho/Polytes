# Polytes - Principles

Polytes is a stickman that lives on your computer. The computer is his home: windows, icons, and everything else are just more objects in the house that he can interact with.

The whole computer is his house: the system, apps, websites, files, everything. If it is on the screen, it is part of his world.

He does not just react to his house: he interacts with it and can change it. The goal is for him to feel like something truly alive inside the computer.

## Pillars

This is how we reach that goal. Every idea should strengthen at least one of them.

1. Animation and Physics: by far the most important pillar. Regardless of the brain, if the animation does not look alive, Polytes does not look alive.
2. Intelligence
   - Personality: attributes that define who he is and how he makes decisions. They come from his name, color, and hollow head. Whether they can change over time is not decided yet.
   - Needs: what he feels and wants to resolve. Maslow's hierarchy of needs is one of the inspirations, used as continuous weights rather than rigid gates: the less satisfied a lower need is, the more it weighs, but it never fully blocks the higher ones. Needs are not limited to Maslow's levels (fear, for example). Underneath it all is hedonism: everything he does ultimately seeks satisfaction.
   - Perception
   - Memory
   - Decision
3. OS integration
